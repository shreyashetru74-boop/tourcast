## Blind test

Tourcast planned a 5-city tour for each artist using only the 3 artists Qloo rates most similar to them, never the artist's own data. The cities were then ranked in the artist's own Qloo fan map (lower is better).

**Across 12 artists, Tourcast's blind picks ranked a median #7 in the artist's real fan map, vs #23.5 for a biggest-cities tour and #240 for a random tour. Tourcast beat the biggest-cities tour for 9 of 12 artists; 43 of 60 blind picks were in the artist's own top 20.**
